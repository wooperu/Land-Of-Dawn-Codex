ARCADE MODES

Beyond The Main Map

Arcade is a hub of rotating and permanent alternative modes — each with its own rules, map, and objective. Some are seasonal and only appear during events. None of them affect your rank. They exist purely for fun, variety, and unique gameplay experiences.

Available modes

🌀
Mayhem
Modified hero skills and special map themes. Only 50 heroes available. Seasonal variants include Halloween, Christmas, and Easter editions.
🪞
Mirror
Both teams vote for a hero — entire team plays the same hero. Start at Level 4 with 900 gold. Duels of same hero, different tactics.
🪂
Survival
33 teams of 3 battle royale-style on the Maze of Minos. Loot by killing jungle creeps. 15 selectable heroes across Physical, Magical, and Defense categories.
💀
Deathbattle
When your hero dies, pick a new one. Win by destroying the base or reaching 30 kills first. No duplicate hero picks allowed.
🍔
Frenzy
Food items spawn around the map granting buffs to hero abilities. Some achievements are exclusive to this mode.
🧬
Evolve
15 players collect gems to level up and evolve. Survive until Level 15 — the first player there becomes the "Devil" and deaths become permanent.
💎
Ravage
5v5 — collect Aquamarines dropped by eliminated enemies. First team to 15 wins. No lanes, no turrets — pure elimination chaos.
♟️
Magic Chess
8-player auto chess. Buy, upgrade, and position heroes to trigger synergies. Last player standing wins.
👻
Shadow Brawl
Team votes for one hero — everyone plays the same. Equipped with a Chain that pulls you back to a marked position every 20 seconds.
🃏
Arena
1v1 card battle. Summon heroes and monsters using Shards and Essence to destroy the enemy Little Commander.
🏰
Chess TD
Tower defense battle royale. 6 players on 6 maps — buy, deploy, and upgrade heroes to defend against waves and rival invasions.
💣
Boommander
Play as Little Commanders and throw explosive Fluffy puffballs at opponents on tile-based maps. Dodge and blast to win.

The ultimate 5v5 MOBA experience on mobile. Team up, strategize, and claim victory in every battle!
