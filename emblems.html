Emblems

Emblems are hero enhancements that grant additional buffs or effects as well as bonus attributes.

In the preparation tab or during the hero pick phase, players can choose their preferred emblem sets.

BASIC COMMON EMBLEM

ATTRIBUTES

Hybrid Regen +12
HP +275
Adaptive Attack +22

STANDARD TALENTS

Thrill

Gain 16 Adaptive Attack.

Swift

Gain 10% Extra Attack Speed.

CORE TALENTS

Impure Rage

Dealing damage with skills deals 4% of target's Max HP extra Adaptive Damage and restores 2% Mana on hit. (If the hero does not use Mana, restores 1% HP instead. The extra Adaptive Damage dealt to non-hero units is up to 120. Cooldown: 5 seconds.)

Quantum Charge

Dealing damage with Basic Attacks increases Movement Speed by 30% for 1.5s and restores 75-180 HP (scales with level). Cooldown: 10 seconds.

War Cry

The next Ultimate cast decreases other active skills' remaining cooldowns by 1.5 times within 4 seconds. This effect is extended by 2 seconds after getting hero kills or assists. (This only applies to the hero themselves. Cooldown: 20 seconds.)

Temporal Reign

The next Ultimate cast decreases other active skills' remaining cooldowns by 1.5 times within 4 seconds. This effect is extended by 2 seconds after getting hero kills or assists. (This only applies to the hero themselves. Cooldown: 20 seconds.)

TANK EMBLEM

ATTRIBUTES

HP +500
Hybrid Defense +10
HP Regen +4

STANDARD TALENTS

Vitality

Gain 225 extra max HP.

Tenacity

When HP is below 50%, Damage Reduction is increased by 5%.

CORE TALENTS

Concussive Blast

After the next Basic Attack, deal 100(+7% Total HP) Magic Damage to nearby enemies. Cooldown: 15s.

ASSASSIN EMBLEM

ATTRIBUTES

Adaptive Penetration +14
Adaptive Attack +10
Movement Speed +3%

STANDARD TALENTS

Rupture

Gain 5 Adaptive Penetration. Gain Physical Penetration if extra Physical Attack is higher than extra Magic Power; otherwise, gain Magic Penetration.

Master Assassin

If there is only one enemy hero nearby, damage dealt to them is increased by 7%.

CORE TALENTS

Killing Spree

When damaging heroes below 30% HP, instantly recover 15% of Lost HP and gains 20% Movement Speed. Additional Effect: Reset cooldown upon killing an enemy hero. Duration: 3 seconds. Cooldown: 30 seconds.

MAGE EMBLEM

ATTRIBUTES

Magic Power +30
Cooldown Reduction +5%
Magic Penetration +8

STANDARD TALENTS

Inspire

Cooldown Reduction is increased by 5% and Mana Regen by 2.

Bargain Hunter

Equipment can be purchased at 95% of their base price.

CORE TALENTS

Lethal Ignition

Dealing damage greater than 7% of an enemy hero's Max HP 3 times within 5s will scorch the target for an additional 162-750 Adaptive Damage (scales with level). Cooldown 15 seconds.

FIGHTER EMBLEM

ATTRIBUTES

Hybrid Lifesteal +10%
Adaptive Attack +16
Hybrid Defense +8

STANDARD TALENTS

Firmness

Gain 8 extra Physical & Magic Defense.

Festival of Blood

Gain 6% Spell Vamp. Each hero kill or assist grants an additional 0.5% Spell Vamp, up to 12 stacks.

CORE TALENTS

Brave Smite

Brave Smite: Dealing skill damage to an enemy hero recovers 5% Max HP. CD: 6 seconds.

SUPPORT EMBLEM

ATTRIBUTES

Healing Effect +12%
Cooldown Reduction +10%
Movement Speed +6%

STANDARD TALENTS

Agility

Gain 4% extra Movement Speed.

Pull Yourself Together

Cooldowns of Battle Spells and equipment's active skills are reduced by 12% initially. Each hero kill or assist grants an additional 1% reduction, up to 8 stacks.

CORE TALENTS

Focusing Mark

After damaging an enemy hero, allied heroes deal 6% more damage to them and gain 10% Movement Speed. Duration: 3 seconds. Cooldown: 4 seconds.

MARKSMAN EMBLEM

ATTRIBUTES

Attack Speed +15%
Adaptive Attack +16
Adaptive Penetration +10%

STANDARD TALENTS

Fatal

Gain 5% Crit Chance and 5% extra Crit Damage.

Weapon Master

Physical Attack and Magic Power gained from equipment, emblem, talents, and skills are increased by 8%.

CORE TALENTS

Weakness Finder

Basic Attacks slow enemies by 50% and reduce their Attack Speed by 30%. Duration: 1 second. Cooldown: 10 seconds (each Basic Attack reduces it bv 1 seconds, down to 3 seconds).
