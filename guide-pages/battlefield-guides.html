BATTLEFIELD GUIDE

Battlefield Guide

Master the map, control objectives, and lead your team to victory.

The Map

Always keep an eye on the minimap. Tracking enemy positions and movement patterns gives your team a major tactical advantage — helping you anticipate fights, rotations, and ambushes before they happen.

The battlefield is divided into three main areas: the three lanes and the jungle in between. Every decision you make — when to push, when to rotate, when to take an objective — should be informed by what you see on the map.

Lanes & Turrets

There are three lanes on the map — Top Lane, Mid Lane, and Bot Lane. Each lane has three turrets. Your goal is to push through enemy minions, destroy all three turrets, and eventually reach and destroy the enemy base to win.

Clear enemy minions quickly to free up time to attack turrets.

Let your minion wave reach the enemy turret before committing — they help soak turret damage.

Never charge a turret blindly. Enemies will rotate to defend and you can easily be caught out.

Always have an escape plan and watch the minimap for incoming enemies.

Once your lane is cleared, rotate to support other lanes rather than staying idle.

Build your minion wave by clearing enemy minions — a large wave will pressure towers on its own.

Jungle

The jungle fills the space between the three lanes and is packed with monsters that grant gold and experience. Jungling is one of the fastest ways to scale your hero in the early game.

Turtle & Lord

These two objectives are among the most impactful in the game and are frequently ignored at lower ranks.

Turtle

Defeating the Turtle grants bonus gold to your entire team, giving you a gold lead over the enemy.

Lord

After defeat, the Lord pushes a lane for you. Group with it to siege towers and the enemy base.

Best time to take an objective: right after winning a team fight or when you catch one or two enemies out of position near the center of the map. Always clear surrounding bushes first and keep Retribution ready as a jungler.

Combat Tips

Protect your jungle early. If you are a roamer or mid laner, watch enemy movement in the first minute and intercept any invasion attempts on your jungler's blue buff.

Stay off the minimap. Appearing too often makes you easy to predict and ambush. Wait in a bush until the next minion wave arrives.

Roamers: keep moving. Provide vision by checking bushes and rotate with your mid laner. Avoid staying glued to your jungler unless they are under pressure.

Turret diving. Only dive an enemy turret if you are significantly ahead or fully confident you can secure the kill. A failed dive opens your lane to a counter push.

Focus on objectives, not kills. Towers win games — kills do not. Players who chase kills while ignoring turrets lose more often.

Praise your teammates. Criticizing poor plays makes things worse. Acknowledge good decisions — it builds team confidence and leads to better play.

Team Fight Positioning

Once the laning phase ends, group up for tower sieges and team fights. Use the "Prepare for Team Fight!" ping to call your team together before engaging.

Frontline

Tanks and fighters engage first. They soak damage, initiate, and create space for your damage dealers.

Backline

Marksmen and assassins wait for the frontline to go in. As a squishy hero, you will rarely out-damage three opponents alone.
