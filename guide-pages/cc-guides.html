CROWD CONTROL GUIDE

Crowd Control Guide

A breakdown of every CC type, how they stack, how they are countered, and how to use them to your advantage.

What is Crowd Control?

Crowd control — commonly abbreviated as CC — is a catch-all term for skills and abilities that prevent or diminish a target's control over their hero, such as the ability to move, deal basic attacks, or cast abilities.

CC effects are superior forms of debuffs because they directly disable a target's combat ability rather than merely limiting it. This makes them invaluable both offensively — locking down enemies during fights — and defensively, as a means of escape or peel.

The term CC effect can refer to two things depending on scale: a control effect when it affects a single target, and a crowd control effect when it can be applied to two or more targets at once. Both terms are commonly used interchangeably.

!

CC effects are organized into four severity levels: Low, Basic, High, and Ultimate. Higher-level CC tends to disable more actions and interrupt more skills — but also becomes harder to apply.

CC Types

There are thirteen crowd control effects in the game, split across four severity levels. Each level defines how many actions are disabled and how many skills can be interrupted when the effect is applied.

Low

Least restrictive. Cannot interrupt skills.

Disarm

Prevents the target from using Basic Attacks. Skills remain usable and the target can still move freely. Does not interrupt any active skills.

Restrain

Prevents the target from leaving a defined area — typically a Circle of Light. The target can still attack and use skills. Does not interrupt active skills.

Root

Prevents the target from using Blink or Charge skills, including Flicker. The target can still attack and use non-mobility skills. Does not interrupt active skills.

Immobilize

Prevents the target from moving and from using Blink or Charge skills. Basic attacks and non-mobility skills remain available. Does not interrupt active skills.

Basic

Disables attacks and/or skills. Can interrupt some skills.

Stun

The most common CC type. Prevents the target from moving, using Basic Attacks, and using skills. Can interrupt some active skills.

Silence

Prevents the target from using skills or Battle Spells, with the exception of Purify. Movement and Basic Attacks remain unaffected. Can interrupt some skills.

Terrify

Prevents Basic Attacks and skill usage, and forces the target to move in random directions. The target cannot control their movement with the joystick. Can interrupt some skills.

Taunt

Forces the target to chase the caster and use Basic Attacks on them. Skill usage is disabled. If the taunter dies, the target becomes stationary for the remaining duration. Can interrupt some skills.

High

Disables all actions. Interrupts a large number of skills.

Freeze

Prevents the target from moving, using Basic Attacks, and using skills. Interrupts a large number of active skills. More disruptive than a standard Stun.

Petrify

Identical in effect to Freeze — prevents movement, Basic Attacks, and skill usage, interrupting a large number of active skills. Visually distinct but mechanically the same.

Airborne

Prevents movement, Basic Attacks, and skills. Interrupts a large number of skills as well as Blink and Charge effects. The target is lifted off the ground for the duration. Ignores Resilience.

Transform

Converts the target into a critter, preventing Basic Attacks and skill usage. Movement is reduced by 50–70% depending on hero level. Interrupts a large number of active skills.

Ultimate

Cannot be removed by Purify or control immunity.

Frozen Moment

Prevents movement, Basic Attacks, and skills — but uniquely does not interrupt active skills or Blink and Charge effects already in progress. Cannot be removed by Purify or control immunity.

Suppress

The most powerful CC type. Prevents all actions and interrupts a large number of skills including Blinks and Charges. Ignores Resilience, control immunity, and cannot be purified.

Stacking CC Effects

CC effects cannot be stacked to extend their total duration. Applying a second CC effect to an already-controlled target does not add time — it replaces the current effect and resets the timer to the new effect's duration.

For example: a target is stunned for 1 second. Halfway through, an Airborne effect with a 2-second duration is applied. The stun is immediately replaced by the Airborne effect, and the timer resets to 2 full seconds — the remaining stun time is discarded.

!

This means applying a weaker CC on top of a stronger one will downgrade the effect. Be mindful of your team's CC order — save high-level CC like Suppress or Airborne for when it will not be wasted overwriting an existing one.

Countering Crowd Control

Several mechanics exist that allow heroes to resist, reduce, or completely negate incoming CC. Understanding these gives you options when building a hero that is vulnerable to being locked down.

Resilience

An attribute that reduces the duration of incoming CC effects by a percentage. Does not work against Airborne, Suppress, or Frozen Moment. Buildable through certain equipment and emblems, making it useful against CC-heavy enemy compositions.

Control Immunity

A temporary state that renders the hero immune to all control effects for its duration — with the exception of Suppress. Heroes under control immunity are highlighted in orange (green for Benedetta), and their username is replaced with "CC Immune." Note that immunity does not remove CC effects already applied before it activated.

Slowing Immunity

A distinct state from control immunity — it only renders the hero immune to slowing effects specifically. Does not protect against stuns, roots, or other CC types. Often granted by specific skills or items.

Untargetability

The most powerful defensive state in the game. A hero that is untargetable cannot be locked onto or affected by enemy Basic Attacks, Battle Spells, or skills — granting full immunity to both damage and CC for the duration. Superior to both control immunity and slowing immunity. Some exceptions may apply to pre-existing debuffs.

►

Use Purify. The Battle Spell Purify immediately removes all CC effects except Suppress. A well-timed Purify can negate an enemy's entire combo.

►

Build Resilience items. Against lineups with multiple stuns or airborne effects, Resilience can meaningfully cut the time you spend disabled.

►

Use CC immunity windows wisely. Many heroes have a brief immunity window tied to their dash or ultimate. Time your mobility skills to escape CC setups.

►

Don't group up carelessly. High-level CC like Airborne and Freeze often have AoE. Staying spread prevents your team from being locked down simultaneously.

Trivia

◆

Fanny is the only hero in the game with no form of crowd control in her kit — not even a slow.

◆

Jawhead was the first hero in the game capable of inflicting CC on his own allies, through his skill Ejector.

◆

Marcel is the second hero able to CC allies, using his skill Golden Hour.

◆
