WHAT IS VS. AI MODE

Safe Environtment

Mistakes here cost nothing. No one reports you, no one flames you. It's the most forgiving space in the game to learn at your own pace.

Battle Against Bots

Vs. AI lets you play a full 5v5 match on the Land of Dawn against AI-controlled opponents. The map, objectives, and mechanics are identical to classic — Turtle, Lord, three lanes, jungle — but the enemies are bots, not real players. No rank, no Credit Score impact.

Rewards Still Apply

You still earn Battle Points, hero fragments, and progress on some daily missions. It's not as efficient as classic or ranked, but it's not empty either.

Difficulty Levels

Easy

Bots move slowly, rarely rotate, and make obvious mistakes. Perfect for complete beginners.

Normal

Bots react to objectives and lane pressure. Good for learning rotations and item builds.

Hard

Bots contest objectives, punish overextensions, and use abilities more aggressively.

The ultimate 5v5 MOBA experience on mobile. Team up, strategize, and claim victory in every battle!
