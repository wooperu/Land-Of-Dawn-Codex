BRAWL MODE

WHAT IS BRAWL MODE

Brawl is a game mode in which you are unable to choose your heroes freely. Instead each player will be given a choice between two random heroes. Fights in this mode happen only in one lane with two turrets defending the base in both teams respectively. Brawl matches usually end in a much shorter time compared to Classic Mode and Ranked. This type of match is prefered for raising lost credit scores or complete tasks.

The ultimate 5v5 MOBA experience on mobile. Team up, strategize, and claim victory in every battle!
