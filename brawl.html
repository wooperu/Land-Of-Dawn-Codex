BRAWL MODE

WHAT IS BRAWL MODE

Brawl is a game mode in which you are unable to choose your heroes freely. Instead each player will be given a choice between two random heroes. Fights in this mode happen only in one lane with two turrets defending the base in both teams respectively. Brawl matches usually end in a much shorter time compared to Classic Mode and Ranked. This type of match is prefered for raising lost credit scores or complete tasks.

No Healing

The Altar no longer heals or regenerates mana. All healing effects are halved. The base no longer replenishes itself — every HP counts.

One Lane, Pure Chaos

Brawl strips Mobile Legends down to its most raw form — a single lane, 5v5 battle with random hero selection. Each player chooses between 2 randomly assigned heroes. No jungle, no side lanes, just constant fighting from the first second.

Hero Selection

You are given a choice between 2 random heroes. Each hero may have an additional buff or handicap applied. You can chat with teammates during selection to coordinate picks.

Starting advantage

All players begin at Level 4 with 2,500 gold. Items can only be bought while dead or before leaving the Altar. Once you leave, you must die to shop again.

OBJECTIVES GUIDE

Frozen Crab

Spawns 0:30

Two crabs on opposite sides. Killing gives HP, gold, and a small shield to nearby allies. Respawns 30s after death.

Healing Rune

Spawns 0:30

Two runes on opposite sides. Heals HP and 40% max mana to all nearby allies. Respawns 35s after pickup. Timer is visible to all.

Barrel

Spawns 0:20

One per side. Makes user briefly untargetable, deals true damage, 15s cooldown. Sound plays for all when picked up.

Super Minions

12:00 mark

Deployed at 12 minutes to prevent drawn-out games. Much stronger than regular minions.
