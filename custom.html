WHAT IS CUSTOM MODE

Full Control Over Your Match

Custom Mode lets you create your own private lobby and invite specific players to fill both teams. You control the map, mode, and settings. No matchmaking, no strangers unless you invite them — just you and the people you choose. No rank points are on the line.

Invite Anyone

Fill slots with friends, guildmates, or AI bots. You can mix real players and bots to fill out incomplete teams — perfect for scrimmages with small groups.

No rank restrictions

Anyone can join regardless of rank. Mythic and Warrior players can be on the same team — great for content creators, friend groups, or community tournaments.
