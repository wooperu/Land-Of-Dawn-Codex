CLASSIC MODE

WHAT IS CLASSIC MODE

NO STAKES

Classic mode is a standard 5v5 match on the Land of Dawn with no ranking points on the line. Wins and losses don't affect your rank, making it the perfect place to experiment, practice heroes, and enjoy the game without pressure.

SAME RULES

Classic follows the same map, objectives, and mechanics as ranked — Turtle, Lord, three lanes, jungle. The only difference is the consequence of losing.

MATCH LENGTH

Average games last 15–25 minutes. Longer than Brawl, shorter than a full ranked session. Great for a quick casual session.

Test new heroes before spending tickets or diamonds in ranked — classic is the safest place to learn a kit.

Warm up before a ranked session. Muscle memory, item builds, and map timing all carry over.

Play with friends of different skill levels without worrying about rank disparity restrictions.

Complete daily missions and events — most missions count classic games the same as ranked.

Recover mentally after a loss streak in ranked. Classic resets the mood without resetting your stars.

The ultimate 5v5 MOBA experience on mobile. Team up, strategize, and claim victory in every battle!
