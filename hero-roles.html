Hero Roles

Every hero has a role to play in Mobile Legends: Bang Bang

Discover the strengths, playstyles, and impact of each role and master the field.

MAGE

Mages wield powerful magic to deal area damage or control the battlefield.

They rely on skills and positioning to maximize their impact.

ASSASIN

Assassins are swift eliminators who specialize in bursting down targets.

They excel at mobility, and high damage, and striking quickly.

MARKSMAN

Marksmen are ranged attackers who deal consistent damage from a distance.

They scale strongly into the late game and are crucial for team DPS.

FIGHTERS

Fighters are versatile heroes with a balance of offense and durability.

They battle on the front lines and adapt to various combat situations.

TANK

Tanks are durable heroes who initiate fights and absorb massive damage.

They protect their allies and control the pace of the battle.

SUPPORT

Support assist their teammates through healing, shielding, or crowd control.

They ensure the team’s survival and create opportunities to win
