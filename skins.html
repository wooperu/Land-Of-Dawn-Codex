Skins

Discover all available hero skins — from Starlight exclusives to rare Collector pieces and crossover collabs from your favourite franchises.

★ STARLIGHT

Starlight Skins

Exclusive monthly and annual Starlight skins available through the Starlight membership. New skin every month!
◆ COLLECTOR

Collector Skins

The rarest and most premium skins in the game. Limited availability with stunning effects and exclusive animations.
⬡ MYTHIC

Mythic Skins

High-tier limited skins with transforming effects and unique recall animations. A symbol of prestige on the battlefield.
⬟ LEGEND

Legend Skins

Premium permanent skins with enhanced visual effects, custom skill animations, and unique entrance effects.
◈ EPIC

Epic Skins

The most popular tier in MLBB. Epic skins feature full model redesigns, custom skill effects, and unique voice lines.
✦ SPECIAL

Special Skins

Mid-tier skins featuring redesigned hero appearances with updated textures and some skill effect changes.
▲ ELITE

Elite Skins

Entry-level paid skins with refreshed looks and minor visual improvements. A great starting point for new collectors.
● Basic

Basic Skins

Common skins usually released with heroes, priced at 269 Diamonds and discounted to 31 Diamonds during launch week.
✕ COLLAB

Collab Skins

Limited crossover skins from iconic franchises — KOF, Transformers, JJK, and more.
