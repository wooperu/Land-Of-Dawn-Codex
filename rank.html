RANK MECHANICS

Stars

Win to gain stars, lose to lose them. The best of the best gets the victory

Star Protection

Earn star protection from win streaks. It prevents losing a star on your next defeat — use it wisely.

Win Streaks

Consecutive wins grant bonus stars. Three wins in a row = extra star. Streaks are reset by a single loss.

Season Resets

Each season ends with a rank reset. Higher your peak, the less you drop. Rewards scale with final rank achieved.
